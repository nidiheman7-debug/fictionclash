      Content Policy
      Fiction Clash is a fan-made matchup app. We don't license or provide official character artwork. All character pictures are uploaded by users, for personal and non-commercial fan use. By uploading, a user confirms the image is their own work, used with permission, or shared for fair fan use — and takes responsibility for it. Anyone can report an image using the flag icon next to it. Reported images are automatically hidden after a few reports pending review. If you hold rights to an image on Fiction Clash and want it removed, contact us and we'll take it down within 48 hours. Contact: fictionclash.app@gmail.com
      Cookie Policy
      Fiction Clash doesn't run any third-party advertising or analytics trackers — nothing here is sold or shared with ad networks. But it does use your browser's local storage, plus Firebase's own sign-in session storage, to remember things between visits, and the rules around cookies cover those too. Here's what we store and why. Essential (always on — needed to deliver something you explicitly asked for): • Your sign-in session • Votes you've cast, matchups/clips you've hidden, and pictures you've uploaded • Your saved profile, Avatar Store redemptions, and equipped decorations/fonts • This cookie choice itself Optional (only saved if you Accept all): • Your chosen accent theme • Whether you've seen the intro screen • Your notification history and read-state Reject non-essential and the app still works fully — those few preferences just won't be remembered on your next visit. Contact: fictionclash.app@gmail.com
